## Introduction

Visual arts is the discipline of seeing clearly, translating three-dimensional spatial experience into two-dimensional visual form, and organizing images to communicate intention. Studying art at a foundational level is not about mastering personal style or mimicking specific styles; it is about acquiring the foundational grammar—line, value, perspective, color, and structural composition—that underpins drawing, painting, printmaking, and visual communication. 

This curriculum is designed for self-directed beginners with no prior formal studio training or portfolio experience. It bypasses institutional requirements such as placement reviews or faculty-gated critiques in favor of self-contained, high-signal resources with clear exercise regimens. It is intended for learners who want to develop disciplined observational and technical capabilities from the ground up, rather than casual hobbyists seeking one-off project tutorials.

The curriculum is structured around two parallel learning tracks:

* **Studio Foundations (Sequential):** The core physical skills build cumulatively. Start with **Drawing Fundamentals** to develop basic line quality, perspective, and proportional sighting. Proceed to **2D Design & Composition Principles** to learn how to organize the picture plane, and then into **Color Theory**, which builds directly on your understanding of tonal values by introducing hue, saturation, and color temperature. **Figure Drawing & Anatomy Essentials** applies these structural principles to the human body, the most demanding test of spatial construction. Finally, **Materials & Painting Foundations** transitions from dry drawing media into wet paint handling, pigment behavior, and brush control.
* **Contextual Foundations (Concurrent):** **Art History Survey** and **Visual Literacy & Critique** do not need to wait until you finish the studio sequence. They should be studied alongside your drawing and painting practice. Developing an informed understanding of historical movements and learning how to formally analyze and critique visual work trains your eye, provides historical context, and helps you evaluate your own studio exercises with greater objectivity.

This curriculum focuses strictly on the universal foundations that every visual artist needs. It deliberately excludes specialized commercial or studio tracks, such as concept art, sequential illustration, digital asset pipelines, printmaking, or professional studio management. Once you have worked through these foundational subjects, you can branch out into specialized subfields in [Advanced Topics](advanced_topics.md), put your skills to work on concrete deliverables in [Projects](projects.md).

## Curriculum

### Drawing Fundamentals

The core skills of observation, mark-making, spatial construction, perspective, and value that form the bedrock of visual art practice.

[Drawabox (Uncomfortable)](https://drawabox.com) - A rigorous, exercise-driven online curriculum that drills line control, spatial awareness, and box perspective through structured assignments.

[Drawing Basics (Stan Prokopenko, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLtG4P3lq8RHHMNwxuVk0IcGRtPGHi4vN9) - A video-based alternative to Drawabox that covers mark-making, basic shapes, and tonal shading through visual demonstrations.

[Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain (Betty Edwards)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781585429202) - A complementary text designed to train observational perception, focusing on contour drawing, negative space, and sighting.

### 2D Design & Composition Principles

The principles governing visual organization on a two-dimensional surface, including balance, rhythm, contrast, focal hierarchy, and pictorial harmony.

[10 Minutes to Better Painting (Marco Bucci, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLLmXZMqb_9sbNLM83NrM005vRQHw1yTKn) - A concise video series that breaks down core compositional mechanics, shape design, value grouping, and focal hierarchy.

[Framed Ink: Drawing and Composition for Visual Storytellers (Marcos Mateu-Mestre)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781933492957) - Builds on the visual principles taught by Bucci, applying value patterns, framing, and dynamic staging to visual storytelling.

[Art Fundamentals: Theory and Practice (Otto Ocvirk et al.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780073379272) - A comprehensive college textbook offering formal theory and structured design problems for learners seeking academic depth beyond the video tutorials.

### Color Theory

The optical and physical principles of color, covering hue, value, saturation, temperature, color relationships, and perceptual interaction.

[Color and Light: A Guide for the Realist Painter (James Gurney)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780740797712) - The primary practical guide on light physics, color temperature, gamut masking, and pigment behavior in realistic painting.

[Interaction of Color: 50th Anniversary Edition (Josef Albers)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780300179354) - A classic experimental companion to Gurney that trains the eye to perceive how neighboring hues alter one another through hands-on paper exercises.

### Figure Drawing & Anatomy Essentials

The study of the human form through gesture, proportional systems, structural volume, and surface anatomy.

[Figure Drawing Fundamentals (Stan Prokopenko, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLtG4P3lq8RHGuMuprDarMz_Y9Fbw_d2ws) - The central video course teaching quick-sketch gesture, volumetric construction using simple forms, and skeletal landmarks.

[Figure Drawing for All It's Worth (Andrew Loomis)](https://archive.org/details/loomis_FIGURE_draw) - A free classic text that complements Proko's video lessons with formal systems for proportions, mannequin construction, and perspective planes.

[Figure Drawing: Design and Invention (Michael Hampton)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780615272818) - A modern textbook alternative to Loomis that breaks down anatomical muscle groups and structural landmarks into simplified functional forms.

### Materials & Painting Foundations

The fundamental handling of wet and dry painting media, including surface preparation, paint application, brush technique, and studio workflow.

[Step-by-Step Acrylic Painting For Beginners [Entire Course] (Jed Dorsey, YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_K49oY0_L7M) - A step-by-step video workshop introducing brush handling, palette layout, value blocking, and paint consistency for beginners.

[Daily Painting: Paint Small and Often To Become a More Creative, Productive, and Successful Artist (Carol Marine)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780770435332) - A complementary, structured guide that expands on studio mechanics, covering oil and acrylic paint characteristics, color mixing, and establishing an effective daily painting routine.

### Art History Survey

A chronological and cross-cultural examination of major art movements, canonical works, and historical contexts from antiquity to the contemporary period.

[Khan Academy Art History (Khan Academy)](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/art-history) - A structured online course offering guided chronological modules, video lectures, and quizzes from prehistory through contemporary art.

[Reframing Art History (Smarthistory)](https://smarthistory.org/reframing-art-history/) - An open-access multimedia textbook alternative to traditional surveys, offering thematic global chapters authored by academic art historians.

[The Story of Art (E. H. Gombrich)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780714832470) - An accessible narrative companion book that synthesizes the major stylistic movements of art history into a cohesive story.

### Visual Literacy & Critique

The critical frameworks and analytical vocabulary used to describe, interpret, and evaluate visual artworks and one's own studio output.

[How to Critique (The Art Assignment, PBS Digital Studios)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9neybpOvjaQ) - A short video introduction outlining the etiquette, constructive frameworks, and vocabulary necessary for art critiques.

[Criticizing Art: Understanding the Contemporary (Terry Barrett)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780073379197) - A structured college textbook that expands on critique methods with concrete models for describing, analyzing, interpreting, and evaluating visual work.

[Ways of Seeing (John Berger)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780140135152) - A seminal critical text on visual literacy, deconstructing how social ideology, reproduction, and historical context shape how we interpret visual culture.
