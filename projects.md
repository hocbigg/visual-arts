## Bind your own hardback sketchbooks

- [How to Bind Your Own Hardback Book (KaptinScarlet, Instructables)](https://www.instructables.com/How-to-Bind-Your-Own-Hardback-Book/) - A 15-step craft walkthrough for creating archival hardback sketchbooks using multi-signature folio stitching, PVA spine gluing, and custom cover casing.
- [How to Make an Accordion Book (irinazcona, Instructables)](https://www.instructables.com/How-to-Make-an-Accordion-Book/) - A step-by-step structural guide to folding, joining, and casing an accordion-fold panoramic sketchbook for sequential drawing.

## Paint your own observational still lifes

- [How to Paint a Simple Still Life using Oil Paints (Will Kemp Art School)](https://willkempartschool.com/how-to-paint-a-simple-still-life-using-oil-paints/) - A foundational oil painting tutorial covering directional single-source lighting, solvent ratios, monochromatic underpainting, and direct color blocking.
- [How to Paint a Still Life in 9 Steps (Samuel Earp)](https://samuelearp.com/how-to-paint-a-still-life-in-9-steps/) - A step-by-step studio guide detailing DIY cardboard shadow-box lighting, tonal composition, value restatement, and modeling volumetric form.

## Paint your own oil portraits

- [How to Paint a Portrait in Oil [5-Part Masterclass] (Will Kemp Art School)](https://willkempartschool.com/how-to-paint-a-portrait-in-oils/) - A five-stage classical portraiture guide covering drawing placement, tonal grisaille blocking, fat-over-lean medium application, facial plane modeling, and final glazing.
- [Portrait Painting Tips (Ben Lustenhouwer)](https://paintingportraittips.com) - A structured lesson archive covering live-model sighting, skin tone temperature mixtures, and feature construction from life.

## Paint your own plein air landscapes

- [“The Approach” – Four Essential Steps to Plein Air Painting (Mitchell Albala)](https://mitchalbala.com/the-approach-four-essential-steps-to-plein-air-painting/) - A master landscape painter's outdoor workflow covering site selection, two-value thumbnail notan studies, monochromatic block-ins, and rapid color application before the light shifts.
- Plein Air Landscape Painting: Your Guide to Getting Started (Artists Network) - search "Plein Air Landscape Painting: Your Guide to Getting Started" on Artists Network for a practical field tutorial focusing on sight-size viewfinders, 30-minute underpainting limits, and outdoor palette management.

## Carve your own linocut prints

- Linocut Printmaking for Beginners: What You Need to Get Started (Emma Jones, Jackson's Art Blog) - search "Linocut Printmaking for Beginners Emma Jones" on Jackson's Art Blog for a step-by-step guide to bench hooks, gouge safety, water-washable relief inks, and baren hand burnishing without a press.
- Linocut Techniques, Tools, Supplies, Ink and Beginner Tips (Christopher Bihuniak, Boarding All Rows) - search "Linocut Techniques, Tools, Supplies, Ink and Beginner Tips" on Boarding All Rows for a printmaker's studio tutorial detailing design reversal, carbon transfer, test proofs, and single-block relief printing.

## Print your own zines

- [How to Make a Zine (Rona Akbari & Somnath Bhatt, The Creative Independent)](https://thecreativeindependent.com/guides/how-to-make-a-zine/) - A complete DIY publishing walkthrough detailing single-sheet 8-page folding templates, physical collage layouts, photocopying mechanics, and saddle-stitch assembly.
- [Zine-Making Resources (Library of Congress)](https://guides.loc.gov/zines/zine-making-resources) - A curated open guide providing printable folding templates, layout blueprints, and archival preservation practices for independent printmakers.

## Draw your own comic strips

- [Step-by-Step Guide: My Comics Process (Palle Schmidt, Comics for Beginners)](https://comicsforbeginners.com/step-by-step-guide-my-comics-process/) - An indie graphic novelist's ten-step studio pipeline covering script writing, thumbnail grid breakdowns, dialogue balloon placement, rough pencil drafting, and ink lettering.
- How to Make a Comic Book in 10 Steps (Blurb Blog) - search "How to Make a Comic Book in 10 Steps" on Blurb for a structural walkthrough of sequential panel pacing, establishing shots, gutter margins, and print-ready digital formatting.

## Illustrate your own character model sheets

- Model Sheets for Character Designers (Julio Robledo, Clip Studio Paint Art Rocket) - search "Model Sheets for Character Designers Julio Robledo" on Clip Studio Paint for a studio guide to head-unit proportion grids, front, profile, and three-quarter turnarounds, and character expression panels.
- [Character Design Challenge (Character Design References)](https://characterdesignreferences.com) - A global, open-submission prompt platform where illustrators design original characters according to monthly narrative constraints and model-sheet requirements.

## Build your own artist portfolio websites

- [How to Make an Artist Website (Amiria Gale, Student Art Guide)](https://www.studentartguide.com/articles/how-to-make-an-artist-website) - An educator-led tutorial on curating artwork portfolios, domain selection, responsive grid layouts, high-resolution photography optimization, and artist statements.
- [How to Make a Free Art Portfolio (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p_oERVzlDvM) - A step-by-step video tutorial demonstrating how to build, customize, and publish a zero-cost, clean visual art portfolio using Google Sites.
